Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. This page covers two things: this website (the Defender's Labs) and the "SecMotion channel agent", a private tool the channel's owner uses to manage the SecMotion YouTube channel.

In short

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data about visitors or viewers. The website has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. The channel agent is used by one person, the channel's owner, on their own Google account only.

This website (Defender's Labs)

  • No accounts and no forms sent to us. Your answers are checked by code running in your own browser. They are never sent to a server.
  • Saved on your device only. So that a lab remembers your answers when you come back, the page keeps them in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser's site data removes them. We cannot see them.
  • No cookies, no trackers, no analytics, no advertising.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to operate and protect its service; see GitHub's privacy statement. We have no access to those logs.
  • The lab data is synthetic. Every name, address, log line and file in a lab is made up for teaching. None of it describes a real person or system.

The SecMotion channel agent (Google sign-in)

The channel agent is a script that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public: the only Google account that signs in to it is the owner's. It uses Google's APIs to do the following on the owner's own channel and calendar.

  • YouTube Data API (upload and manage videos): upload the owner's own videos, set their titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails and playlists, and read and reply to comments on the channel's videos.
  • YouTube Analytics API (read only): read the channel's own statistics, such as views, watch time and audience retention, to improve later videos.
  • Google Calendar API (only calendars the agent itself created): keep a release calendar of upload dates. It cannot read the owner's other calendars.

What data it handles, and where it goes

  • Stored locally only. The sign-in token, the channel's statistics and the text of public comments on the channel's videos (with the commenter's public display name) are kept in files on the owner's computer. There is no server and no database operated by us.
  • Not shared, not sold, not transferred. Data obtained through Google's APIs is not given to any third party, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Used only for the purposes above. The agent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Its use of YouTube follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Retention and deletion. Local copies are kept only as long as they are useful for running the channel and can be deleted at any time by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which makes the stored token useless.

Children

The site and the channel teach cybersecurity to a general audience. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
